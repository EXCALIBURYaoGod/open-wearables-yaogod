## What does this change?

A sleep session may now start with `awake` samples, and the "first sample must be a sleep stage" rule is applied to the merged night instead of to each SDK upload. An awake-only upload no longer writes anything; it stays pending until the upload that brings the rest of the night.

## Why?

A night delivered in several uploads lost the awakening that opened a later piece, so a real interruption disappeared and the night looked uninterrupted (#1899).

Keeping the awakenings in a stored record and fixing them on a later merge would have meant rewriting a night that another upload already reported, and in the cross-device case refiling it under the relaying device. Letting the upload wait keeps the record written once, by the first upload that contains sleep. The trade-off is that an awake-only state sits in Redis until the next upload; the TTL discards it if none comes.

Related issue: #1899
